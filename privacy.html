Matrix Focus

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Matrix Focus works without an account. If you never sign in, your tasks and settings stay on your device and are never sent to us.

What we collect when you sign in

Signing in is optional and only used to sync your data between your devices. When you sign in we store:

  • Account details: your email address, and your name if you sign in with Apple or Google and they share it. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your email; we then only see Apple's relay address.
  • Your tasks: titles, notes, quadrant, completion state, scheduled dates and times, and when each was created, changed or deleted.
  • Synced settings: your quadrant names and daily goal. Theme, sound and other display settings stay on the device.

We don't collect analytics, advertising identifiers, location or contacts, and the app has no ads or trackers.

How we use it

Only to sign you in and keep your tasks in sync across your devices. We don't sell or share your data, and we don't use it for advertising or profiling.

Where it's stored

Account details are kept in Amazon Cognito and synced data in Amazon DynamoDB, both run by Amazon Web Services in the United States (Oregon). Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Apple and Google handle their own sign-in under their own privacy policies; we receive only the details listed above.

How long we keep it

Synced data is kept while your account exists. When you delete a task, a deletion marker is kept for up to 90 days so your other devices can remove it too. Backups are kept for up to 35 days.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete Account. This immediately deletes your account and all synced data from our servers (backups expire within 35 days). Signing out removes the account's tasks from that device. You can also ask us to delete your data via the contact link below.

Children

Matrix Focus isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact
